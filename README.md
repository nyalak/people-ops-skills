# People Ops Skills for Claude Code

Modern hiring tools for People leaders — built on the **8 Key Relationships Framework**.

## What's included

### `/work-experience-and-interview`

Generates two documents in one workflow:

**1. Work Experience Description (WED)**
A modern alternative to the traditional job description. Built on the 8 Key Relationships framework — helping candidates holistically envision themselves in the role, not just read a list of tasks.

**2. Full Structured Interview Plan**
Includes recruiter screen, hiring manager interview, competency interviews with level-calibrated questions, optional values round with tailored questions, role exercise brief, and a master candidate scorecard.

### Leveling frameworks supported

**Pave**
- Individual Contributors: Support 1–3, Level 1–6
- Managers and Leadership: M3, M4, M5, M6, E7, E8, C-Suite
- 46 job families with sub-families

**Carta**
- Individual Contributors: L1 (Associate I) through L9 (Fellow)
- Managers and Leadership: Team Lead through C-Suite
- 19 job families

### Other features
- Values interview round — paste your company values and get tailored behavioral + situational questions per value
- Competency count auto-calibrated by level (2 for IC1–IC3/L1–L3, 3 for senior IC and all management levels)
- 1–4 scoring scale (Strong No / No / Yes / Strong Yes) throughout all scorecards
- Works with Claude Code, Codex CLI, Cursor, and Gemini CLI

## The 8 Key Relationships Framework

Every Work Experience Description is structured around 8 key relationships that shape how a person feels throughout their journey with an organization:

1. **The People I Work With** — who I serve, manage, collaborate, partner, and engage with
2. **The Technology I Use** — the technology that connects me to my work, workforce, and workplace
3. **The Places I Work** — where I do my work and the physical space in which work gets done
4. **The Sense of Belonging** — the inclusive community my organization creates
5. **The Work I Do** — my work, my ways of working, the norms and methods I use
6. **How Work Affects My Life** — my wellbeing, compensation, rewards, and how my worthiness is reflected
7. **The Organization I Work For** — the mission, purpose, culture, and leadership behaviors
8. **How I Grow as a Human** — the education, experiences, and exposure that support my growth
