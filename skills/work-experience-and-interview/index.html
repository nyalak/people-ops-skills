The 8 Key Relationships framework

The traditional job description lists tasks and qualifications — but says nothing about what it actually feels like to work somewhere. Every Work Experience Description is structured around the 8 relationships that shape how a person feels throughout their journey with an organization.

01

The people I work with

Who I serve, manage, collaborate, partner, and engage with

02

The technology I use

The technology that connects me to my work, workforce, and workplace

03

The places I work

Where I do my work and the physical space in which work gets done

04

The sense of belonging

The inclusive community my organization creates

05

The work I do

My work, my ways of working, the norms and methods I use — and how my work strengthens me

06

How work affects my life

My wellbeing, compensation, rewards, and how my worthiness is reflected by the organization

07

The organization I work for

The mission, purpose, culture, and leadership behaviors

08

How I grow as a human

The education, experiences, and exposure that support my growth and give me purpose
